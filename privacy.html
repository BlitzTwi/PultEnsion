Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026
Applies to: PultEnsion, a Chrome browser extension (version 1.0.0 and later)
Contact: blitztwi@gmail.com

Short version

PultEnsion has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. While it is installed it makes no network requests at all. Everything it stores stays inside your own browser, and nothing is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.

There is exactly one exception, and it happens only after you remove the extension: Chrome opens a short, entirely optional feedback form. See After you uninstall.

What PultEnsion stores

PultEnsion needs to remember your setup in order to work. All of it is written through Chrome’s own chrome.storage API:

In chrome.storage.sync — Chrome may copy this between your own signed-in Chrome profiles

Data Why it is needed
Group names you type To label your groups in the popup
The Chrome extension IDs you place in a group To know what a group’s switch should turn off
The Chrome extension IDs you mark “Always on” To make sure those are never switched off
Your appearance choice (system / light / dark) To render the interface you picked
The list filter you last used To reopen the popup the way you left it
A timestamp recording that the welcome tour was shown So the tour does not open again

In chrome.storage.local — this device only, never synced

Data Why it is needed
The extension IDs that were enabled just before you switched a group or the master switch off So that switching back on restores exactly your previous setup instead of enabling everything

That is the complete list. PultEnsion does not read, collect, store or transmit:

  • your browsing history, the URLs you visit, or the content of any page;
  • personally identifiable information — no name, e-mail address, account or device identifier;
  • authentication data, cookies, form input, financial or health information;
  • any file on your computer;
  • usage statistics, telemetry, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

PultEnsion contains no third-party code, no SDKs, no trackers and no remote code. It does not run on web pages: it has no content scripts and no access to website data.

What PultEnsion does with the management permission

The management permission lets PultEnsion read the list of extensions installed in your browser (name, icon, ID, enabled state) and switch extensions on and off when you ask it to. That list is used only to draw the popup and to carry out your clicks. It is never copied out of the browser.

PultEnsion never disables an extension installed by your organisation’s policy, never touches themes or Chrome apps, and never disables itself.

Chrome sync

If you have Chrome sync switched on, Google copies chrome.storage.sync data between your signed-in Chrome profiles. That transfer is performed by Chrome itself under Google’s Privacy Policy; the developer of PultEnsion has no access to it and receives nothing from it. The record of “what was on before” is deliberately kept in local storage only, because it describes one specific computer.

Data retention and deletion

  • Deleting a group or removing an “Always on” star deletes that data immediately.
  • Uninstalling PultEnsion removes its stored data from your browser. If Chrome sync is on, the removal propagates to your other signed-in profiles.
  • There is nothing for the developer to delete, because the developer never receives your data.

After you uninstall

When you remove PultEnsion, Chrome opens one page in a new tab: a short feedback form, hosted on Google Forms, asking why you removed it. It is the only way the developer can hear from the people who left.

  • Answering is voluntary. Closing the tab sends nothing.
  • The extension sends nothing. It is already uninstalled by then. Chrome itself opens the address, registered in advance through chrome.runtime.setUninstallURL, and nothing from your groups, your “Always on” list or your settings is attached to it.
  • If you do answer, your answers go to Google Forms and are then covered by Google’s Privacy Policy as well as this one. The form does not ask for your name or your e-mail address; please do not type anything into it you would rather not share.
  • Answers are read only to decide what to improve. They are never sold, never shared and never used for advertising.

Limited Use disclosure

PultEnsion complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Allowed use. The data described above is used only to provide the extension’s single purpose — switching groups of extensions off and back on.
  • Allowed transfer. No user data is transferred to anyone. There is no server, no third-party service and no export of data by the extension.
  • Prohibited advertising. No data is used or transferred for advertising, personalisation, re-targeting or interest-based profiling.
  • Prohibited human interaction. No human, including the developer, can read your data: it never leaves your browser.

Children

PultEnsion is a general-purpose developer/productivity tool. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Security

Because no data leaves your device, there is no transmission to secure. Local data is protected by Chrome’s own extension storage isolation: no website and no other extension can read PultEnsion’s storage.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of PultEnsion changes what it stores, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be summarised in the extension’s Chrome Web Store listing.
